6 Questions ◆ April 2026 Events ◆ ~3 Minutes
Question 01 / 06 💼 Deal of the Month

Santander UK completed a landmark acquisition on 30 April 2026. What was the deal, and what made it historic?

Correct: B. Santander UK completed its £2.65bn acquisition of TSB on 30 April 2026 — the single largest investment in UK banking in over 15 years. The combined group now serves nearly 28 million customers, making Santander UK the third largest bank by personal current account balances and fourth in mortgages.
Question 02 / 06 🍺 Pub Quiz Bonus

TSB is expected to vanish from UK high streets following the Santander takeover. Roughly how long had TSB been part of British banking history?

Correct: C. TSB traces its roots back approximately 215 years to the early savings bank movement of the 19th century. Its expected disappearance from the high street marks the end of a remarkably long chapter in British retail banking.
Question 03 / 06 🏦 Neobank Watch

On 1 April 2026 — and no, it wasn't a prank — which UK neobank announced it was shutting down its US operations after seven years, making around 50 employees redundant?

Correct: C. Monzo announced on 1 April 2026 it was exiting the US, calling it "a deliberate, strategic decision to focus on scaling in our home market and Europe." Monzo had entered the US in 2019 via a partner bank but never secured its own US licence. Ironically, rival Revolut filed for a full US national banking licence that same month.
Question 04 / 06 🏦 Neobank Watch

As Monzo retreated from the US in April 2026, which UK neobank was simultaneously doing the opposite — filing for a full US national banking licence?

Correct: B. Revolut filed for a US national banking charter with the OCC in early 2026 — the same month Monzo quit the market entirely. Revolut already holds its UK banking licence, reported a record $2.3bn profit before tax for 2025, and serves over 68 million customers globally.
Question 05 / 06 📋 Regulation

The FCA and PRA confirmed reforms in April 2026 designed to halve the administrative burden on financial firms. Which long-standing regime were they overhauling?

Correct: B. The FCA and PRA's first phase of SM&CR reforms in April 2026 gave firms more time for senior manager applications, updated financial thresholds for Enhanced firms, and removed unnecessary complexity — while keeping the core principle of senior leader accountability firmly intact.
Question 06 / 06 🤖 AI & Innovation

The Bank of England's Financial Policy Committee raised concerns about AI in financial services in April 2026 — and asked regulators to urgently investigate one specific type. What was it?

Correct: B. The FPC's April 2026 record warned that while AI hasn't yet reached systemic risk levels, risks are likely to increase "potentially rapidly." It specifically asked the Bank and FCA to investigate agentic AI — systems that autonomously take multi-step actions — focusing on payment and financial market use cases.
