Question 01 / 06📈 Monetary Policy

The Bank of England held Bank Rate at 3.75% throughout May 2026. On 29 May, Governor Andrew Bailey gave a clear signal about the path ahead. What did he say?

Correct: B. On 29 May 2026, Bailey signalled rates would stay at 3.75% at least through the summer — tolerating temporarily elevated inflation (running at 3.3%) to support a softening real economy, but stressing that stance would change if price pressures became more permanent. The Iran conflict's energy price shock had derailed earlier hopes of rate cuts in spring 2026.

Question 02 / 06💼 Wealth Management

NatWest's £2.7bn acquisition of Evelyn Partners moved closer to completion in May. What would the combined AUM be, and which heritage NatWest private bank does it sit alongside?

Correct: B — £128bn alongside Coutts. Evelyn's £69bn AUM combines with NatWest's £59bn to create the UK's largest private banking and wealth management business, sitting alongside Coutts. The deal is expected to boost NatWest's fee income by ~20% — a deliberate pivot toward higher-margin, capital-light revenue as interest rates gradually fall.

Question 03 / 06📋 Regulation

The Financial Services and Markets Bill, introduced to Parliament on 19 May, is the government's most significant financial regulation shake-up in years. Beyond abolishing the PSR, what does it do to the Senior Managers & Certification Regime (SM&CR)?

Correct: B. The Bill removes the Certification Regime from SMCR's primary legislation — not abolishing the accountability framework, but giving the FCA and PRA more flexibility to update requirements through rules rather than waiting for primary legislation. Senior manager accountability remains intact. The government also shortened application deadlines (senior manager approvals from 3 months to 2) as part of the broader growth agenda.

Question 04 / 06📋 Regulation

HM Treasury's Ring-Fencing Review was published on 18 May 2026. Which five banks are directly in scope of the ring-fencing regime and therefore most affected by the reforms?

Correct: A. The five banks in scope of ring-fencing — Lloyds, NatWest, HSBC, Barclays and Santander UK — are those holding significant retail deposits that require the legal separation of retail and investment banking activities. The reforms aim to make the regime more flexible and proportionate while keeping core depositor protections, and could unlock up to £80bn in additional business lending.
Question 05 / 06📋 Consumer Redress

The FCA confirmed its motor finance redress scheme in May and lifted its pause on complaint handling from 31 May. Around how many deals does the FCA estimate are eligible for compensation?

Correct: C — around 14 million deals. The scheme covers car loans sold between 2007 and 2024 where customers weren't properly told about broker commissions. Lenders including Lloyds, Barclays and FirstRand have already set aside billions in provisions. The FCA ended its complaint-handling pause two months earlier than originally planned — on 31 May rather than 31 July — to avoid consumers waiting any longer.
Question 06 / 06🎲 Wildcard

Away from the banking desk — the UK's hottest ever May day on record was broken on 25 May 2026 during the bank holiday weekend. Where was it recorded, and what temperature?

Correct: B — 34.8°C at Kew Gardens. The May bank holiday weekend smashed the previous UK May record (32.8°C, set in both 1922 and 1944) by a full 2°C — and also broke the all-time hottest bank holiday Monday record. Hopefully you noticed from somewhere other than your desk. ☀️
